What's New in Magnifier

Version 1.3.0 Release Notes & Features

v1.3.0

🎉 Welcome to Magnifier v1.3.0!

Thank you for using Magnifier. Here is everything new in this release, designed to give you the ultimate text, image, document, and desktop screen magnification experience.

🌐

System Language Auto-Detection

Magnifier now automatically matches your browser and Windows system language by default across 10+ languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Hindi, Portuguese, Italian, Russian).

🖥️

Standalone Windows Desktop App

Need magnification outside the browser? Download Magnifier.exe — a 100KB portable Windows app with 60 FPS flicker-free screen capture, system tray icon, and global hotkeys.

⌨️

Ctrl+M Global Shortcut

Easily toggle the magnifier ON/OFF anytime by pressing Ctrl+M on your keyboard. Includes a clear status banner and interactive shortcut configuration.

📄

Built-in Localized PDF Viewer

Open and magnify local PDF files seamlessly with our integrated PDF viewer featuring smooth canvas rendering and custom zoom levels.

⭕

Custom Shapes & Positions

Choose between circle and rectangle lens shapes, 3 lens sizes (Small, Medium, Large), 1× to 10× zoom, and 5 cursor anchor positions.
